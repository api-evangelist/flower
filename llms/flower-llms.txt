# Flower

> Flower Labs builds infrastructure for collaborative, decentralized AI. Its flagship open-source framework, Flower (`flwr`), is a friendly, framework-agnostic federated AI framework used to train and fine-tune models on distributed, privacy-sensitive data across organizational boundaries. Flower Intelligence extends this with on-device inference SDKs that hand off to a confidential remote-compute service when needed. Backed by Felicis and Northzone (and Y Combinator). This llms.txt was generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the public Flower Labs surface; flower.ai does not publish its own llms.txt.

## Products
- [Flower framework (flwr)](https://flower.ai/docs/framework/): Open-source federated AI framework (Python), Apache-2.0. Works with PyTorch, TensorFlow, Hugging Face, scikit-learn, JAX, XGBoost, and more.
- [Flower Intelligence](https://flower.ai/intelligence/): On-device AI with optional confidential remote compute; SDKs for TypeScript/JavaScript, Kotlin, and Swift.
- [Flower SuperGrid](https://flower.ai/): Federated AI / collaborative machine-learning network infrastructure.
- [Flower Hub](https://flower.ai/apps): Community deployment platform for ecosystem apps (Flower App Bundles).
